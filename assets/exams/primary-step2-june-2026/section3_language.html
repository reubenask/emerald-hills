Answer Key — 10 Errors in Sam's Big Race

1every → Every (Capitalization — starts a sentence)
2saturday → Saturday (Capitalization — proper noun / day of week)
3sam → Sam (Capitalization — proper name)
4love → loves (Subject-verb agreement — He loves)
5dog → dogs (Plural — two dogs)
6bottle → bottles (Plural — three bottles)
7win? → win. (Punctuation — statement, not question)
8run → ran (Past tense — Last week)
