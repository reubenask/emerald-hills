10 Questions
Read the story. Choose the best answer to each question. You may look back at the story.

A Book for the Class

On Monday, Anna brought a book about birds to school. Her grandfather had given it to her for her birthday. At lunchtime, she showed it to her friend Tom in the library. Tom liked the pictures, but he could not read some of the long words. Anna helped him. Then their teacher, Miss Green, asked Anna to show the book to the class on Friday. Anna felt nervous because she had never spoken in front of everyone before. Tom offered to hold the book while she talked. They practised together after school. On Friday, Anna told the class about three birds. Everyone listened carefully, and Anna smiled.

The Rainy Picnic

On Saturday morning, Jack and his family planned a picnic by the lake. Dad packed sandwiches, and Jack put apples in a bag. His little sister Emma brought a red ball. When they reached the lake, dark clouds covered the sky. Soon it began to rain. Mum saw a small shelter near the trees, so the family went inside. They ate their food there and watched ducks swimming on the lake. Emma was disappointed because she wanted to play with her ball. Jack made up a guessing game to cheer her up. After lunch, the rain stopped. The children played on the grass while Dad took photos. Before going home, everyone picked up their rubbish.

He points to it and smiles. The children enjoy looking after the garden. They hope the plants will grow tall and strong. Before they leave, they pull out some weeds and collect their tools.
