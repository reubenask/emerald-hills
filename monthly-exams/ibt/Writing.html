Task 2 - Write an Email

7 Minutes
You have 7 minutes. Write in complete sentences.

Your student club booked a room for a weekend workshop. The booking confirmation shows the wrong date, and your invited speaker is available only on the original date. Write an email to Ms. Carter, the campus room coordinator.

In your email:

  • Explain the booking error and give the intended date.
  • Describe why changing the workshop date would be difficult.
  • Request a solution and suggest an alternative room if necessary.
To: Ms. Carter
Subject: Workshop room booking

Task 3 - Academic Discussion

10 Minutes
You have 10 minutes. Write at least 100 words. State your stance, support it with reasons and an example, use linking words, and finish with a conclusion.
Professor Lee:

Some universities are considering a policy that allows students to use AI tools when preparing assignments, provided that students disclose their use and check the information. Others would restrict these tools to protect independent thinking. Which approach should universities take, and why?

Maya:

I support allowing these tools with clear rules. A student could use AI to explore different explanations, then compare them with course materials. This could help students who need additional support.

Daniel:

I favor restrictions. If students depend on generated answers, they may avoid the difficult thinking that helps them learn. Teachers may also struggle to judge what a student actually understands.

Contribute your own view to the discussion. Develop your argument rather than copying either student.
